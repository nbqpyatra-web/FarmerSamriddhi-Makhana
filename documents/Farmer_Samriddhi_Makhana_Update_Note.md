## 1. Introduction

Farmer Samriddhi is a QCI initiative aimed at improving farmer incomes, reducing export rejections, and enabling market access through quality interventions across three pilot crops: Makhana (East India), Black Pepper (South India), and Psyllium Husk (West India).

Makhana (foxnut) is the flagship pilot crop under this initiative. QCI-NBQP's Makhana team initiated a fresh engagement with the Directorate of Horticulture (DoH), Government of Bihar — with no prior institutional relationship between the two organisations — which has since grown into an active collaboration to support the crop's overall growth. ICAR-NRCM (National Research Centre for Makhana), Darbhanga, has supported the initiative as a research and event partner. The collaboration is bilateral, between QCI and DoH, Government of Bihar.

## 2. Activities undertaken so far

- QCI-NBQP Makhana team initiated a new connect with DoH, Bihar and undertook research, field visits, and stakeholder identification and meetings across the Makhana value chain.
- Gunvatta Manthan event conducted at ICAR-NRCM, Darbhanga on 17 July 2026, bringing together key stakeholders on quality aspects of Makhana.
- Based on the research, meetings, and the Manthan event, QCI-NBQP Makhana team identified and shared the first set of proposed areas of intervention.
- QCI-NBQP Makhana team subsequently set up a meeting with Shri Abhishek Kumar, Director, DoH, presenting the full scope of collaborative activities to support the crop's overall growth.
- DoH, Government of Bihar sent formal communication in the form of a proposal to Secretary General (SG), QCI vide Letter No. 3064 dated 05 August 2026.
- First round of verbal communication completed between SG, QCI and Director, DoH.
- 15+ areas of intervention identified and mutually agreed upon, spanning 6 QCI boards/divisions: PPID, NBQP, PADD, SPD, TCB, and NDIE.
- Activity tracker created by QCI-NBQP Makhana team, discussed and approved with all involved boards across multiple rounds of meetings, and presented to SG for go-ahead.
