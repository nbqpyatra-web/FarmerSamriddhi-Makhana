## Data points requested from the Directorate of Horticulture, Bihar for TCB Interventions (WS-09/10/11)

### Section 1: Number of Persons to Be Trained
*Board to provide estimated eligible population and proposed training targets, both overall and district-wise.*

Columns: Stakeholder group | Estimated eligible persons | Target persons to be trained | No. of batches | Batch size / notes

Stakeholder groups: Farmers / primary producers; Harvesters / primary processors; Processing-unit owners / supervisors; Processing-unit workers / operators; FPOs / SHGs / cooperatives; Quality-control personnel; Laboratory analysts / sample collectors; Inspectors / field officers; Board officials; Entrepreneurs / aggregators / exporters; Master Trainers (Training of Trainers); Other (please specify); TOTAL

### Section 2: District-wise Training Targets
Columns: District / block | Stakeholder group | Eligible population | Persons to be trained | No. of batches | Priority / remarks (blank rows for DoH to fill)

### Section 3: Training Needs and Technical Priorities
Columns: Training area | Current gap / problem | Target group | Priority (High/Medium/Low) | Expected outcome

Training areas: Cultivation, harvesting and post-harvest handling; Processing, roasting, popping, grading and sorting; GHP, GMP and HACCP principles; Food safety, hygiene and contamination prevention; Packaging, labelling, storage and shelf life; Sampling and makhana testing parameters; Laboratory quality management / ISO/IEC 17025; GI authenticity, traceability and product integrity; Value addition, branding, market access and exports; Management, regulatory compliance and quality governance; Master Trainer Programme; Other (please specify)

| Theme | Question |
| --- | --- |
