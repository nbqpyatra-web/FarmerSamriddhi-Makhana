# Farmers Samriddhi – Makhana: Project Charter
*Text copy of `Project Charter- Makhana Farmers Samriddhi.docx` — QCI-Farmers Samriddhi | Project Charter | September 8, 2026*

## 1. Project Details

### Business Need / Project Objectives
Makhana (foxnut) cultivation is concentrated in Bihar, but the value chain currently suffers from inconsistent quality standards, limited traceability, and recurring export rejections — constraining farmer incomes and market access. Farmer Samriddhi, QCI's flagship initiative to improve farmer incomes, reduce export rejections, and enable market access through quality interventions, has identified Makhana as its pilot crop for East India.

The objective of this project is to establish and operationalize a structured, bilateral collaboration between the Quality Council of India (QCI) and the Directorate of Horticulture (DoH), Government of Bihar, to:
- Strengthen quality standards and certification frameworks across the Makhana value chain
- Improve farmer-level practices through targeted, board-wise interventions
- Enhance market access and reduce export rejections for Bihar-origin Makhana

Formalize the collaboration through a bilateral MoU between QCI and DoH, Bihar.

### Project Requirements
- Stakeholder alignment across QCI leadership (Secretary General, CEO–NBQP) and DoH Bihar (Director, Horticulture)
- Cross-functional participation from 6 QCI constituent boards/divisions: PPID, NBQP, PADD, SPD, TCB, and NDIE
- Board-wise concept notes defining scope, activities, and resourcing for each of the 15+ identified intervention areas
- A consolidated activity tracker to monitor commitments, timelines, and board-level ownership
- Field-level inputs from research/event partners, including ICAR-NRCM, Darbhanga
- Technical and financial due diligence ahead of MoU finalization (to be discussed during the Patna site visit)
- Formal bilateral MoU between QCI and DoH, Government of Bihar, defining roles, scope, and governance

### Project Description / Deliverables
- A signed bilateral MoU between QCI and DoH, Government of Bihar
- Consolidated, board-wise concept note(s) covering all identified intervention areas
- An approved activity tracker with defined ownership across the 6 QCI boards
- Structured stakeholder engagement outputs (e.g., Gunvatta Manthan event outcomes)
- A phased implementation roadmap for quality, traceability, and market-access interventions across the Makhana value chain in Bihar

### Project Does Not Include
- Black Pepper (South India) and Psyllium Husk (West India) pilot crops under Farmer Samriddhi — these are tracked as separate, independent workstreams
- Any tripartite arrangement involving ICAR-NRCM or other research bodies as MoU signatories — the collaboration is strictly bilateral between QCI and DoH, Government of Bihar
- Direct funding, subsidy disbursement, or financial support to farmers — QCI's role is limited to quality, technical, and institutional interventions, not financial assistance
- On-ground implementation or execution of interventions by QCI directly — implementation ownership rests with DoH, Bihar and relevant state-level agencies, with QCI providing technical guidance and frameworks
- Finalization of MoU terms, financial commitments, or legal/contractual aspects — these are to be discussed and concluded separately during the Patna site visit and subsequent MoU process

## 3. Project Considerations

### High-Level Risks
- Delay in board-wise confirmations or concept note collation could push back the DoH submission timeline
- Meet the requirement of DoH's PMU composition criteria and resource on-boarding
- Stage wise distribution of all the intervention to be clarified and accepted by DoH & QCI mutually.

### Acceptance Criteria
- Bilateral MoU between QCI and DoH, Government of Bihar formally signed and executed
- All 15+ agreed intervention areas have board-wise concept notes finalized and accepted by DoH
- Activity tracker approved and adopted as the joint monitoring mechanism by both parties
- Technical, financial, and process terms for implementation confirmed during/post the Patna visit
- No unresolved disagreements on scope, resourcing, or governance between QCI and DoH at MoU signing

### Assumptions
- DoH, Government of Bihar will continue to designate Shri Abhishek Kumar (or an equivalent authority) as the primary counterpart throughout the engagement
- All 6 QCI boards (PPID, NBQP, PADD, SPD, TCB, NDIE) will remain available and committed to their respective intervention areas
- No major change in leadership or mandate at DoH or QCI that would require renegotiation of the collaboration's scope
- ICAR-NRCM will continue to be available as a research/event partner for future engagements (e.g., Manthan-style events), even without MoU signatory status
- Timelines shared by DoH (e.g., concept note review, MoU discussions) reflect their genuine internal capacity

### Constraints
- Collaboration is strictly bilateral (QCI–DoH, Bihar); no third-party institution can be brought in as an MoU signatory
- MoU finalization is contingent on the outcome of the Patna visit (post 15 September 2026) — timelines cannot be firmed up until then
- Resource and financial commitments are subject to approval processes within both QCI and the Government of Bihar, which may have independent timelines
- Scope is not limited to the 15+ mutually agreed intervention areas and 6 identified QCI boards — any changes require fresh mutual agreement
