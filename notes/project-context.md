# Farmer Samriddhi – Makhana: Project Context (updated 19 Nov 2026)

## Purpose & context
- Farmer Samriddhi is QCI's initiative in the agriculture sector, aimed at improving quality at the grassroots level across agriculture and allied sectors. By strengthening the value and supply chain, it aims to improve farmers' incomes and help boost export trade.
- Pilot crops: Makhana (East India), Black Pepper (South India), Psyllium Husk (West India). Each crop has its own SPOC.
- The Makhana MoU is **bilateral**: QCI and the Directorate of Horticulture (DoH), Government of Bihar. (An earlier tripartite plan was dropped.) ICAR-NRCM is a research/event partner (hosted Gunvatta Manthan), not an MoU signatory.

## Project origins (from the initial Gantt chart, Dec 2025 — reference only, dates not aligned with current plan)
Original title: "QCI: Boards & Divisions Relevant to Agriculture Supply Chain Reform"; chart dated 20 Dec 2025; initial owners NBQP, PADD, NABL (PPID joined later).
- **Stage 1 – Agri supply chain study (Jan–May 2026, completed):** SPOCs identified; region and crop (Makhana) selected; secondary research; stakeholder mapping (farmers, traders/exporters, government); market gap analysis; district-wise POCs; agri-expert consultation; questionnaire prepared.
- **Stage 2 – On-site gap analysis / go-to-market visit (13–15 May 2026, completed):** FGDs with farmers, traders and exporters; consultation with ICAR scientists.
- **Stage 3 – Government and regulator meetings (May–Aug 2026, partly completed):** ICAR leadership and NRCM scientists, FSSAI (QA Division), BIS and Legal Metrology, Commissioner Horticulture (GoI), NITI Aayog agri-expert; steering committee constitution started.
- **Stage 4 – QCI's interventions (initial list):** training and curriculum, marketing and promotion, farmer training, 4-level seminars/conferences, IT and data integration, Export Excellence Award, Gunvatta Manthan (held 17 Jul 2026). These later evolved into WS-01 to WS-14.
- **Stage 5 – Next steps:** meeting with Director Horticulture (held 29 Jul 2026) and proposal; a tripartite MoU was planned here — later dropped for the bilateral QCI–DoH MoU.
- **Stage 6 – Expected outcomes:** export rejection reduction, farmer income increase, inter-state gap comparison, new market penetration.

## Scope
- 15+ areas of intervention, mutually agreed, spanning 6 QCI boards/divisions: PPID, NBQP, PADD, SPD, TCB, NDIE.
- SG pack (Sep 2026): (a) Excel mapping Letter 3064 problem statements to delivery workstreams (15 rows, P1–P9 → WS-01–WS-14 with lead boards); (b) deck with 14 workstreams, 5 pillars, 3 stages / 5 gates over 36 months, 10-person PMU under PPID (WS-08).
- Letter 3064 = 9 support areas + dedicated manpower request (Sec B) + way forward (Sec C) + Annexure I (10 items) + 17-page Concept Note.
