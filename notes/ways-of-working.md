# Ways of Working

## Principles
- Verify, don't invent: anything unconfirmed gets a marked placeholder.
- Reference documents (e.g., a reference MoU or prior deck) are for structure/style only.
- Surface judgment calls for approval rather than deciding unilaterally.
- Avoid implying criticism of senior government officials' sector management.
- Keep distinct entities separate (e.g., state DoH vs. central Makhana Board under DA&FW).
- Map figures to legitimate government sources (APEDA, PIB, Union Budget documents, GI Registry); flag unverified figures.
- In trackers, flag unresolved questions rather than resolving them independently.

## Workflow
- Formal government correspondence: approve an outline before full drafting.
- Internal communications: brief, plain language.
- Trackers, concept notes, Project Charter: drafted as text for review; formatted files (Excel tracker, Word MoU, PPTX deck, RoD) when a deliverable is required.
