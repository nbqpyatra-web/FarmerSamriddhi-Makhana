## Purpose & context
- Rachit works on QCI's NBQP team on the Farmer Samriddhi – Makhana initiative, a bilateral collaboration between QCI and the Directorate of Horticulture (DoH), Government of Bihar, focused on quality interventions across Bihar's makhana (fox nut) value chain.
- Rachit is also referred to internally as Shivam Shukla, Analyst, NBQP at Quality Council of India.
- Farmer Samriddhi definition: "QCI's initiative in the agriculture sector, aimed at improving quality at the grassroots level across agriculture and allied sectors. By strengthening the value and supply chain, the initiative aims to work to improve the farmer's incomes and help boost the export trade."
- Farmer Samriddhi pilot crops: Makhana (East India), Black Pepper (South India), Psyllium Husk (West India).
- Rachit leads the overall Farmer Samriddhi project and is also the Makhana SPOC; Psyllium Husk and Black Pepper each have their own separate SPOC.
- Makhana MoU is bilateral only: QCI and Directorate of Horticulture (DoH), Govt of Bihar. There is no tripartite MoU — that was an earlier plan, now dropped. ICAR-NRCM is a research/event partner (hosted Gunvatta Manthan) but not an MoU signatory.
- ICAR in Makhana–DoH reports: past activities (e.g., Gunvatta Manthan at ICAR-NRCM, Darbhanga, 17 Jul 2026) may be mentioned as history. Do not include ICAR/ICAR-CIPHET in current status, upcoming tasks or way forward unless Rachit explicitly asks.

## Ways of working
### Principles
- Verify, don't invent: if something is not confirmed, leave a marked placeholder and ask.
- Reference documents are for structure/style only; do not copy or rework their content unless told otherwise.
- Surface judgment calls for approval rather than deciding unilaterally.
- Avoid implying criticism of senior government officials' sector management.
- Keep distinct entities separate (state Directorate of Horticulture vs. central Makhana Board under DA&FW).
- Map figures to legitimate government sources (APEDA, PIB, Union Budget, GI Registry); flag unverified figures.
- In trackers/documents, flag unresolved questions rather than resolving them independently.
- Corrections to project facts are standing corrections carried into all future work.
### Workflow
- Rachit shares documents before assigning the day's task and expects context to be retained.
- Formal government correspondence: approve an outline before full drafting.
- Internal communications: brief, plain language, with an optional fuller version.
- Trackers, concept notes, Project Charter: text in chat for review; files only when a formatted deliverable is required.
